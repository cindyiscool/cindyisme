🌑 ACT I: The Dark Thorns of Self-Doubt

Do I actually enjoy learning... or am I too focused on proving that I’m capable?

I always set high goals, wanting to do well in academics and pursue opportunities. Yet, sometimes I feel confused even about myself:

“Do I actually enjoy learning, or am I too focused on proving that I’m capable?”

I feel happy when I study (in some specific parts, I guess), but… when I fail, I never dare to trust myself again. But who is the one I’m trying to prove? I don’t really care about my parents or people around me. But then, do I have to let external factors affect my own trust for myself? Then what am I?

While I think that I am ambitious, the idea of constantly switching the goalpost feels like every achievement becomes meaningless, yet not meeting it is horrible. Then when is the time that...

📍

“Constantly shifting the goalpost makes every achievement feel hollow, yet falling short terrifies me to the point of losing all faith in myself…”

🥀 ACT II: The Paralysis Before the Finish Line

“There is no rose without a thorn.”

Nevertheless, there is no rose without a thorn. While ambitious, I don’t have the ability to trust myself. In any last step of achieving new achievements, I always freak out and am not able to perform as usual.

⚡
The Invisible Panic: Sometimes it does matter, but most of the time it affects my results and then I always think that I am not able to do anything.
📜
During a Scholarship: While everything presented in front of me are the things that I can do in a few seconds, I still freak out and cannot complete it.
💭
Right at this moment: Or just like right now, I start worrying about my school score even though everything is completely fine!

🌸 ACT III: The Blooming Garden

Living in a plain but comfortable life or a harsh but accomplished one?

“Living in a plain but comfortable life or a harsh but accomplished one?”

I don’t like a plain life. I am looking for a life that I can look back on and know that I did not settle for what was comfortable simply because it was comfortable.

✨

“I don't know yet how harsh that journey will be, or whether every goal I have now will remain mine. But I know that I would rather struggle toward something I deeply care about than wake up one day wondering what I could have become.”

💖

“No matter how many thorns line the path ahead, I still choose passion. Because that is the only way my own rose will truly blossom.”
